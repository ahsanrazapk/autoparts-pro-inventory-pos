AutoParts Pro

Inventory and point-of-sale for auto-parts shops in Pakistan. One app, many shops, each with its own data.

  • Multi-shop: every shop's data is kept separate, with admin, manager and cashier roles
  • Offline-first POS (PWA) keeps selling when the internet drops
  • Urdu/English receipts, 4 invoice templates, payment ledgers and WhatsApp invoices
